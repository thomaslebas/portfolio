# Pokémon Go Fieldbook

Subline: To catch them is my real test
Meta: AR gaming · Data visualisation · Vibe coding

I've been playing Pokémon GO since it launched in 2016. Nearly a decade of catches, walks, and raids across New Zealand, Canada, the UK, and more.

Fieldbook turns the collected game data into something usable: a personal trainer dashboard with catch history, a map of every unique encounter location, and my full Pokémon collection. It becomes something I can share with other players. Knowing what I've caught tells you what Pokémon I'm missing, what I might be able to trade, and where I've played.

Timed, as it happens, with the game's 10th anniversary.
