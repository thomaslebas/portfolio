# Pokémon Go Fieldbook

Subline: To catch them is my real test

I've been playing Pokémon GO since it launched in 2016. Nearly a decade of catches, walks, and raids across New Zealand, Canada, the UK, and more.

Fieldbook turns that data into something usable: a personal trainer dashboard with catch history, a map of every unique encounter location, and my full Pokémon collection. 

It's also the latest in a long series of personal data projects I've made since university, family trees, travel maps, medical history, usually as static posters. This is the first one that's interactive, shareable, and publicly available. Timed, as it happens, with the game's tenth anniversary.
