# Sad Boy

Subline: A SAD lamp that talks back

In the British winter of 2025, I found a SAD lamp that looked like it had potential… I soon found it was calling for a name, a face, a voice. Reflecting on my new favourite tools, Cursor and ChatGPT, an idea spawned. What if I could make this lamp into a real boy? A real Sad Boy.

The goal: bring Sad Boy to life with a digital face and ability to converse with me. I had no technical background in hardware or audio pipelines, so I took the idea to ChatGPT and asked it to help me break it into something buildable, piece by piece. That in itself became the project.

Sad Boy's guts is a Raspberry Pi running a voice pipeline, mic in, speech-to-text, language model, text-to-speech, speaker out, all headless, all directed without me writing a line of code. It was my first time calling AI APIs directly, and my first time building something with a physical dimension rather than a screen. 

A fun and quirky way to light up my winter.
