# Dialogues with Tomorrow 2010
Year: 2011
Subline: A speaker series and book bringing cultural perspective to the climate crisis
Meta: Editorial design · Book design
Thumbnail: spread-1

Dialogues with Tomorrow 2010 was a speaker series bringing a cultural perspective to the imminent climate crisis. It was a bold attempt to mix disciplines, asking people from very different worlds to meet on stage and either find common ground or talk across a gulf. The book is the culmination of these dialogues, held between contributors of various disciplines and experiences—artists, cultural historians, scientists and economists. An online version was also developed hand-in-hand with the printed book.
