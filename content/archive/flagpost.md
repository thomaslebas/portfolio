# Flagpost
Year: 2015
Subline: An online platform for voting and discussion during New Zealand's flag referendum
Meta: Product design · Civic tech
Thumbnail: photo-1

Flagpost was an online platform that enabled voting, discussion, and tagging of design submissions during the New Zealand Flag Consideration Process. It was used to facilitate informed decisions, dialogue, and open collaboration at a national scale. Visitors to the site used tags to help find designs relevant to their interests and values, voted to give their preferences, and contributed to discussion threads that enabled critique and ongoing conversations around specific designs.

Stats:
- An initiative of the Design+Democracy Project, in collaboration with Springload
- Designed at Massey University, Wellington
- Silver, New Zealand Best Awards 2016
