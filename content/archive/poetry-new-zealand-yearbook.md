# Poetry New Zealand Yearbook
Year: 2017
Subline: A new cover system for New Zealand's longest-running poetry magazine
Meta: Editorial design · Book design
Thumbnail: spread-3

Poetry New Zealand is the country's longest-running poetry magazine, showcasing new writing from Aotearoa and overseas. It presents the work of talented newcomers and developing writers as well as that of established leaders in the field. This issue is the first in a new direction for the publication, forming a foundation for the system of naming and format, with the celebration of letterforms as the central parameter for cover design.

Stats:
- Designed for Massey University Press (MUP)
- In collaboration with Jo Bailey and Fay McAlpine
