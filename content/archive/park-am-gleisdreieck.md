# Park am Gleisdreieck
Year: 2013
Subline: Environmental graphics and a map for a historic railway-junction-turned-city-park
Meta: Environmental graphics · Wayfinding
Thumbnail: map-1

A historic railway-junction turned wasteland, the Gleisdreieck area has now become a modern city park with unique character and features. With the eastern side of the park opened in 2011, the western park was in need of a variety of environmental graphics to complement it. Continuing on from the existing graphic system of the eastern park, a map was developed to finally present the park as a whole.

Custom pictograms were used for certain areas, and feature prominently in the physical space, with additional pictograms used in play areas. Large-scale typography and arrows were applied to entrances and a large walkway joining the two sides of the park.

Stats:
