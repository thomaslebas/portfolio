# Old School New School Timeline
Year: 2011
Subline: An exhibition and timeline marking 125 years of the Wellington School of Design
Meta: Exhibition design · Brand identity
Thumbnail: installation-1

This exhibition was commissioned in 2010 to showcase the contribution of the Wellington School of Design to the fabric of New Zealand over its 125 year history. Through design objects, artifacts, and associated stories, the exhibition traverses the history of New Zealand art and design. In addition to the design of the visual identity, a timeline was designed to be at the core of the exhibition, emphasising the long history by identifying each exhibitor's time spent at the school.

Stats:
- Designed at Massey University, Wellington
