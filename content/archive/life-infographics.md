# Life Infographics
Year: 2011–20
Subline: An ongoing series of infographic posters charting my own life
Meta: Information design · Poster design
Thumbnail: poster-6.png

The project is a continuous series of infographic posters, presenting information from my own life. To date, the project covers my family tree, assessment data from my Bachelor's, my overseas experience, my medical history, and my family's origins across Europe compared to what my DNA suggests.
