# Opening Night
Year: 2017
Subline: A poster system for a monthly live music night, reflecting each night's lineup
Meta: Poster design
Thumbnail: poster-1

Opening Night sees a range of new local musicians performing live, premiering brand new material, and putting a fresh spin on old formats. Each Opening Night offers a different flavour and gives future musical storytellers the opportunity to cut their teeth in a live music environment.

In collaboration with Harry Pickernell, a poster system was developed to take into account the varying flavours and musicians performing at each monthly event.
