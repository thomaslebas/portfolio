# Te Awahou Nieuwe Stroom
Year: 2015
Subline: A brand to connect a new cultural centre with its community, region, and history
Meta: Brand identity
Thumbnail: mark-1

Te Awahou Nieuwe Stroom is a multi-purpose facility being constructed in Foxton, New Zealand. It provides opportunities for the local community to make it their home, as well as a place for locals and visitors to experience the mix of regional and historical culture, including a home for two museums of Māori and Dutch history within the region. Te Awahou Nieuwe Stroom required a brand to help activate the stories and relationships of the area—past, present and future—and enhance the connectedness of the centre to Foxton, the region, New Zealand and the world.

Stats:
