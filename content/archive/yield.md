# Yield
Year: 2011
Subline: An interactive exhibition for zero-waste fashion design, bridging gallery and website
Meta: Exhibition design · Interaction design
Thumbnail: installation-2

Yield was a fashion design exhibition containing the work of passionate New Zealand and international fashion designers who have a radical ambition—to make fashion without making waste. As a way to modernise the exhibition experience, interactive touch screens and QR codes were used to aid and expand the viewers' understanding of the garment and designer, by connecting to content on the Yield website.

In collaboration with Holly McQuillan, Timo Rissanen, Chris Jackson, and Gerbrand van Melle

Stats:
- Shown at the Dowse Museum, Wellington, 2011
- Shown at the Textile Arts Centre, Brooklyn, New York, 2011
