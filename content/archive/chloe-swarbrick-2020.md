# Chlöe Swarbrick for Auckland Central 2020
Year: 2020
Subline: Campaign graphics for Chlöe Swarbrick's 2020 election win in Auckland Central
Meta: Political campaign · Poster design
Thumbnail: poster-1

Proudly helped produce graphics for Chlöe Swarbrick's 2020 campaign to become Auckland Central's local MP, as part of the wider election campaign of the Green Party of Aotearoa New Zealand. Against odds, Chlöe won, becoming the second Green Party MP to win an electorate seat in the history of the party.

Stats:
- Designed in Auckland
- In collaboration with the campaign team
