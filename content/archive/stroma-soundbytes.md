# Stroma: Soundbytes
Year: 2012
Subline: A poster system for Stroma's Soundbytes concert series, driven by instrumentation and colour
Meta: Poster design
Thumbnail: poster-1

Stroma is New Zealand's largest and most flexible chamber ensemble. Focusing on music written in the last 100 years, Stroma performs with a repertoire of fresh, cutting-edge compositions. Inspired by this unique ensemble structure and contemporary interpretation of classic music, a poster system was developed for their Soundbytes series. Each event consisted of different instruments and music style, which in turn governed the instruments and colour featured on the individual event posters.

Stats:
- Designed at Open Lab, Wellington
