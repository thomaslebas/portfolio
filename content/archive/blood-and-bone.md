# Blood and Bone
Year: 2010
Subline: A concept platform for archiving and exploring family history
Meta: Concept · Interaction design
Thumbnail: screen-1

Our family records are not only prone to damage and loss but are becoming more scattered and difficult to share in our globalised world. How can our priceless artefacts, memories and information be archived to a safer, more connected and engaging environment? Blood and Bone was my answer to this question: a platform for families to document and explore their family's history, in a way that modernises the archival process while amplifying the experience we can make with our heritage and family story.

Stats:
