# Our Data, Our Way
Year: 2017
Subline: A board game and tool turning data-sharing scenarios into everyday decisions
Meta: Product design · Civic tech
Thumbnail: screen-1

Our Data, Our Way was developed to enable a broad cross-section of New Zealanders to easily express their personal positions on a range of hypothetical data scenarios. A board game and online tool were developed by translating social licence terminology into an everyday, commonsense decision tool, letting New Zealanders express their positions on different data use and sharing situations, and asking what would make them more comfortable with each scenario.

Our Data, Our Way involved 27 face-to-face workshop conversations across New Zealand from Whangarei to Invercargill, involving 379 participants, and the online tool received over 4,000 responses. ourdataourway.nz
