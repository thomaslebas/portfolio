# Die Zusammenarbeiter
Year: 2013
Subline: A responsive website portal for a Berlin architecture collective
Meta: Web design
Thumbnail: screen-1

'The Co-workers', as their name translates, are a Berlin-based architecture studio. Their work combines program development, process control, and architecture, and in keeping with their open network concept they like to form project-based collaborations with the Berlin community.

Based on the visual identity developed at SV Associates, a responsive website design was required to complete this visual transition. The website was envisioned to be a portal to many of their projects and the communities around it, while also providing an overview of their work and philosophy, with a hope of appealing to the like-minded.

Stats:
- Designed at SV Associates, Berlin
- zusammenarbeiter.de
