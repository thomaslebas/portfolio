# Kakapo: The Evolution Story
Year: 2010
Subline: Classroom posters explaining the kākāpō's evolutionary story
Meta: Information design · Poster design
Thumbnail: poster-1

The Kakapo is the rarest parrot in the world and has developed through an extremely isolated evolutionary process. With the population at 122 the Kakapo are at their highest numbers since 1979. It is through understanding evolutionary processes of the Kakapo that has led to the success of its conservation. These posters are intended as classroom resources to better understand their evolutionary processes.
