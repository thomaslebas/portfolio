# Gore® Fabrics
Year: 2013
Subline: Simplifying and harmonising a sprawling brand family across Gore's fabric technologies
Meta: Brand identity · Brand architecture
Thumbnail: system-5.png

W. L. Gore & Associates Inc. products are ubiquitous in everyday life. They can be found in medical implants, cables, filters, seals, membranes, ventilation, and fiber technologies for a wide variety of industries—and, of course, as laminates for high-performance protective and sportswear. Given the multitude of applications, the Gore brand architecture, which had evolved over the decades, became increasingly complex. The brand family of the Fabrics Division, the company's core business area, was ultimately barely recognizable by end consumers—too many heterogeneous design elements overshadowed the shared DNA.

This work significantly simplified the complexity of the Fabrics brand family in terms of its architecture, and harmonized the design to make the affiliation of the family members visible and understandable.
