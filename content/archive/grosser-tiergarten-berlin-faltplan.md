# Großer Tiergarten Berlin Faltplan
Year: 2014
Subline: A folded visitor map for Berlin's largest and most historic urban park
Meta: Environmental graphics · Map design
Thumbnail: map-1

Historically rich and vibrant, Großer Tiergarten is Berlin's largest urban park. The park is a tourist attraction for its wealth of memorials and historical monuments, most notably the Große Stern with the Siegessäule at its center. Gradually being restored to its former pre-war glory, the park has undergone a rebranding to breathe life back into itself. Upgrades to signage and maps were sorely needed, and the rebranding is reflected in them—a mix of new and old, paying respect to the physical heritage of the park while maintaining a vintage appearance synonymous with the park's design.
